Before we start.

Common questions about scope, packages and the way production is handled.

Can I hire you for only one service?+
Yes. Editing, scriptwriting, thumbnail design, SEO, AI production, uploading and channel optimization can be requested separately.
What is included in the complete workflow?+
Depending on the package: research, scripting, AI production, editing, color, SEO, uploading and channel optimization.
Can you produce AI documentaries and fictional films?+
Yes. The current portfolio includes both AI documentary-style work and an AI-generated fictional movie created with VEO 3.
Can the documentary format be adapted to other languages?+
Yes. The same production format can be adapted for other language markets, subject to the project's scripting, translation and voiceover requirements.
Are AI generation credits included in the monthly price?+
No. Third-party AI generation credits and platform usage fees are separate and are paid directly by the client.
Do you guarantee views, subscribers or monetization?+
No. YouTube performance depends on many factors. The service focuses on the production work and deliverables that can actually be controlled.
Are the prices fixed?+
The listed prices are standard package rates. They are negotiable depending on project scope, complexity, volume and requirements. Contact me for a custom quote.
